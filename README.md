# ApexAssure-Group
Analyzed 381K existing health insurance customers to evaluate current business performance and optimize a vehicle insurance cross-sell strategy. By using SQL &amp; Power BI uncovering key behavioural trends, I identified high-value target customers to maximize sales conversion and minimize customer acquisition costs.

# The Business Problem

Cross-selling vehicle policies to our base of 381,109 existing health insurance customers represents a massive revenue opportunity. However, indiscriminate outbound marketing resulted in bloated Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC) and call-center fatigue, with a baseline conversion rate of just 12.26%. This project leverages SQL, Python, and Power BI to transform a scattergun sales approach into a highly targeted, data-driven lead generation engine.

# Methodology & Key Insights

By analyzing demographic trends, behavioral indicators, and channel unit economics, I identified the primary drivers of purchase intent. Key findings include:
The Inertia Barrier: Customers with existing auto coverage almost never switch mid-cycle. Filtering them out eliminates 45.8% of wasted outreach volume while preserving 99.6% of all potential buyers.

The Emotional Catalyst: 97.9% of conversions came from customers with prior vehicle damage, proving past out-of-pocket loss is the core trigger for purchasing coverage.

The Revenue Engine: Policyholders aged 36–50 generated nearly half (49%) of all target premium ($724.8M) with an industry-leading 21.3% conversion rate.

Channel Efficiency: By creating a custom "Revenue Yield per Contact" metric, I uncovered a major budget leak in Channel 151 (3.1% conversion, $928/lead) and recommended redirecting capital to VIP luxury channels yielding up to $8,069 per contact.

# Business Impact & Deliverables

Deliverables include an interactive Power BI dashboard for regional sales operations and a set of validated feature recommendations passed to senior data scientists for future machine learning development. The resulting analytics strategy focuses manual sales efforts exclusively on the "Golden Segment," projecting a capture of over 80% of cross-sell revenue while cutting outbound marketing costs by nearly 50%.
